## 1. What Sealed is

Sealed is a workout habit tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch with one honest
ritual: at the end of the day you press a wax seal on your day, and the
impression only forms as complete as you earned.

Three arcs feed the day:

- **TRAIN** — workouts, set by set. Hit the set at target? One tap. Off plan?
  Slide in real plates (weights step in plate increments, dumbbells in rack
  gaps, machines in stack pins). You never type a number. A rest timer runs on
  the lock screen.
- **FUEL** — name your meals once, then each day is a tick, not a diary. Macros
  optional (UK CoFID food data, barcode scan). Drinks count; alcohol is scored
  plainly, never lectured.
- **BASE** — the quiet stuff Apple Health already knows: steps, sleep, active
  energy, rings, stand hours, water. Fills itself.

When an arc completes, its wax melts. When the day's done, you press.

**The seal:** hold the wax once a night and the impression forms to the
percentage you earned. **80% holds the day and the run.** Beat the plan and the
wax comes up **gilded**. **Sealed days lock forever** — no retro-edits. Runs
earn metals: brass at a week, bronze at a fortnight, silver at a month, platinum
at two, gold at three. Wax, lava or liquid, your colour (real Metal shaders —
the seal responds to touch and tilt).

**The Pact:** two people, one streak, advancing only on nights you both hold
the day. Send a pump. Turn on the duel — always kind: "Alex edged it 94 to 91",
never "you lost". Bad start? Call day one again, together.

**The Words:** every Sunday evening a coach reads your week and writes back —
short, specific, yours — with suggested uplifts to weights and reps.

Also: the Wall (every seal kept, honest gaps included), Progress charts (weight,
tape, estimated 1RM by lift), widgets + lock-screen widgets, a real Apple Watch
workout with a seal complication.

**Kind by design:** scheduled rest days seal by design; one missed day a week
can be mended; no loser language; no dashboard shouting at you.

**Private by design:** everything lives on the phone. No ads, no tracking, no
email address. Health data never leaves the device.

**£3.99 a month, 7-day free trial. One plan, everything included.** No free
tier, no yearly plan, no tiers. Never say "free app", "free tier" or "free
plan". "7 days free" / "free trial" is correct. Everything you sealed stays on
your phone whether or not you subscribe.

## 3. Audience and the emotional pitch

Gym-goers and lifters (strength, PPL-style plans), UK-leaning, iPhone + Apple
Watch, iOS 18. Habit-streak people and accountability pairs. People who've found
every other tracker *flattering* — "the number at the end of the day was
whatever I wanted it to be."

The feeling: finality, pride, ritual. You complete the day and make it final.
Honest **but kind** — accountability without shame. 80% is a good day.

Founder note (Sean, solo dev, UK — can be used first-person when the angle is
"founder"): *I'd used every tracker going and they all had the same problem:
the number at the end of the day was whatever I wanted it to be. I wanted
something I couldn't argue with. A seal felt right — it's an old idea, it's
final, and it's beautiful when it's earned.*

## 4. Voice

Terse, warm, never preachy. Short punchy lines. Plain English, UK spelling.
Confident, not hype. Example of the register: "Ring's open. 92% earned today —
seal it."

**Words we use:** earn, the close, seal, press, hold, impression, wax, gilded,
lock, ritual, arcs, pact, honest, kind by design, private by design, the wall,
the run, TRAIN / FUEL / BASE (caps when naming the sections).

**Never:** loser/failure language, "you lost", lectures, "crush it", "grind",
"beast mode", "no excuses", calorie guilt, gamified hype, "spreadsheet energy",
emoji walls, exclamation-mark stacks, "game-changer", "revolutionary",
"AI-powered". No fake urgency. No made-up stats or testimonials.

**Facts you may state as true:** everything in sections 1–2; "every frame is
the real app" (we only ever post real screen captures); "built solo in the UK";
"the wax is a Metal shader". Do not invent user numbers, ratings, awards or
features not listed above.

## 6. Platform rules

**Instagram** (feed post / carousel of screenshots, caption ≤ 2,200 chars, aim
300–700). Hook on line 1 — it's the only line shown before "more". Line
breaks between ideas. CTA: "Link in bio" (the bio link goes to the Shop Grid /
site). **3–5 hashtags, small ones, on the last line.** Core set: `#gymtok
#fitnessapp #habittracker #applewatch #workoutapp`; rotate in `#gymhabits
#dailyhabits #trainlog #liftlog #workoutlog`. Never more than 5.

**TikTok** (photo post from screenshots; `title` ≤ 90 chars shown bold on the
post, `caption` ≤ 2,200 but aim 120–300 — TikTok readers skim). Title = the
hook. Caption conversational, one idea, ends with a soft CTA ("Sealed, on the
App Store" / "iPhone + Apple Watch"). **3–5 hashtags inline at the end** —
`#gymtok` and `#fitnessapp` almost always, plus 1–3 from the rotation above.

**Threads** (text-first, ≤ 500 chars, aim 120–280; **no hashtags in the
text** — a single topic tag is set separately). Casual, human, first-person
is fine. Ends with either a soft CTA ("Out now for iPhone and Apple Watch") or
an open question — questions get replies on Threads. Topic tag candidates:
`fitnessapp`, `habittracker`, `gymtok`, `applewatch`, `indiedev`.

**Angles** (the dashboard passes one; lean into it):
- `hook` — the sharpest single idea the screenshot proves. Product-forward.
- `honest` — the "can't lie to yourself" truth-telling angle. Finality, locks,
  real numbers, 80% holds.
- `founder` — Sean's first-person voice. Why it was built, the craft (Metal
  shader, solo, UK), what the screenshot shows from the maker's side.
- `pact` — two people, one streak. Accountability without shame. Tag-a-friend
  energy without saying "tag a friend".
- `feature` — explain exactly what's on screen (TRAIN plates, FUEL ticks, BASE
  signals, the Wall, the Words) in plain terms. Educational.
